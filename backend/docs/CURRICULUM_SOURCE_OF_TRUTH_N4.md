# Curriculum Source of Truth - N4

Tai lieu nay la hop dong noi dung giua SumaryJapanese va Gemini cho cap do N4.
Gemini khong duoc tu suy dien chu de, ngu phap hay so luong tu moi; moi module
phai duoc tao tu mot dong syllabus da duoc khoa trong tai lieu nay.

## 1. Nguyen tac nguon tham chieu

Su dung ba lop nguon theo thu tu uu tien:

1. **JLPT competency target**: muc tieu ky nang nghe, doc va nhan biet tu vung/
   Kanji/ngu phap o cap do N4.
2. **Giao trinh tham khao**: Minna no Nihongo (I, II), Irodori va Genki dung
   de tham khao thu tu su pham, tinh huong va do kho; khong sao chep nguyen van
   bai, audio, vi du, hinh anh hay bai tap co ban quyen.
3. **Syllabus cua SumaryJapanese**: danh sach pham vi bat buoc ben duoi. Day la
   nguon quyet dinh khi cac giao trinh khac nhau ve thu tu hoac cach chia bai.

## 2. Tien quyet N5

Nguoi hoc N4 da hoan thanh toan bo 12 module N5 va dat it nhat 70% o hai mock
test N5 lien tiep. Tat ca tu vung, Kanji, ngu phap da hoc trong N5-01 den N5-12
duoc coi la `da biet` va co the su dung tu do trong vi du, reading, listening va
quiz cua N4 ma khong can chu thich.

Pham vi N5 da hoc (tom tat):
- khoang 600-700 tu vung;
- 100 Kanji;
- khoang 80 mau/cach dung ngu phap;
- masu-form, te-form co ban, tinh tu i/na, chi thi, so dem, thoi gian;
- doc Hiragana va Katakana thanh thao.

## 4. Muc tieu tong the N4

Sau khi hoan thanh 14 module N4, nguoi hoc co the:

- su dung thanh thao plain form va polite form;
- dieu khien cac the dong tu: potential, passive, conditional;
- giao tiep trong cac tinh huong doi song thuong ngay;
- doc email, thong bao, huong dan don gian (200-400 ky tu);
- nghe hoi thoai 45-90 giay o toc do vua;
- viet email/nhat ky 120-180 chu;
- dat khoang 1,500 tu vung luy ke;
- nhan biet khoang 300 Kanji luy ke;
- dat tu 70% o hai de N4 lien tiep.

## 5. Goi du lieu bat buoc cho moi module N4

Moi module phai co mot file brief rieng (dua tren template
`N4_CONTENT_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md`), khong tao truc tiep tu prompt chung.
File brief can khoa:

- `code`, `level`, `unit`, tien quyet va learning outcomes;
- grammar allowlist va grammar forbidden list;
- vocabulary domains, quota core/extension va danh sach tu da hoc;
- Kanji moi, readings duoc phep va tu ghep bat buoc;
- boi canh Kaiwa, do dai Listening/Reading, dau ra Writing;
- quota tung bang va mapping section;
- tieu chi dat module va cau hoi QA;
- JSON schema, enum, zero-based answer va placeholder audio.

### Dinh muc noi dung cho mot module N4

| Loai | So luong muc tieu | Bang/model |
|---|---:|---|
| Module | 1 | `LearningModule` |
| Section | 10 | `LessonSection` |
| Vocabulary | 50-80 | `Vocabulary` |
| Kanji | 10-16 | `Kanji` |
| Grammar | 6-9 | `GrammarPattern` + `GrammarExample` |
| Kaiwa | 2 | `Conversation` + `ConversationLine` |
| Listening | 2-3 | `ListeningContent` |
| Reading | 2 | `ReadingPassage` |
| Writing | 2 | `WritingTask` |
| Lesson exercises | 35-45 | `LessonExercise` |
| Module quiz | 20 | `QuizQuestion` |

## 6. Quy trinh dung Gemini de tao dung N4

Khong yeu cau Gemini tao ngay mot JSON lon. Dung hai luot:

### Luot A - Lap ke hoach

Gui syllabus row, cumulative ledger (bao gom tat ca N5 da hoc) va brief. Yeu
cau Gemini tra ve `plan.json` gom danh sach tu vung, Kanji, grammar,
dependencies va canh bao trung lap.
Nguoi review phe duyet plan truoc khi tao content.

### Luot B - Tao content

Chi sau khi plan duoc phe duyet, yeu cau Gemini tao JSON theo brief. Bat buoc
ghi `sourceRef` cho moi item (vi du `N4-04.vocab.013`) va `introducedIn`/
`reviewedIn`; khong tao database ID.

Sau do chay validator may va mot vong review tieng Nhat. Gemini khong duoc tu
dat status `PUBLISHED`; du lieu moi luon la `DRAFT`.

## 7. Prompt khung cho Gemini - N4

```text
Ban la content designer cho SumaryJapanese.

Cap do hien tai: N4 (Giao tiep doi song)
Tien quyet: Nguoi hoc da hoan thanh toan bo N5 (12 module).

Chi duoc su dung:
1) CURRICULUM_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_N4.md;
2) file brief cua module (N4_CONTENT_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md da dien);
3) cumulative-ledger.json do du an cung cap (bao gom tat ca N5 + N4 da hoc).

Khong duoc tu them grammar, Kanji hoac tu vung ngoai allowlist. Neu can them,
dua vao `extensionCandidates` va dung tu da biet thay the. Khong sao chep
nguyen van Minna no Nihongo; hay viet vi du, hoi thoai va passage moi.

Moi item phai co sourceRef, introducedIn/reviewedIn, explanation va level.
Khong tao id, moduleId, SQL, password, token hay URL audio that.
Status luon la DRAFT. correctIndex va correctAnswer dung zero-based.

Luu y N4 khac N5:
- Tu vung phong phu hon (50-80 tu/module).
- Kanji nhieu hon (10-16/module).
- Ngu phap phuc tap hon (6-9 mau/module), co conjugation va comparison.
- Listening dai hon (45-90 giay), toc do 0.85-1.0.
- Reading dai hon (200-400 ky tu).
- Writing yeu cau viet doan 120-180 chu, co bo cuc.
- Exercise/quiz nhieu va da dang hon (35-45 bai tap, 20 quiz).

Tra ve JSON duy nhat theo schema cua brief. Neu co bat ky dieu nao khong chac
chan, dung tao du lieu im lang; ghi vao `qaWarnings`.
```

## 8. Cac lop QA truoc khi import

1. **Schema**: JSON parse, enum, required fields, foreign-key mapping.
2. **Curriculum**: dung allowlist, dung quota, khong trung cumulative ledger.
3. **Tieng Nhat**: reading, pitch/romaji, chia dong tu (plain/potential/passive/
   conditional), tu nhien va cap do.
4. **Su pham**: learning outcomes xuat hien trong exercise/quiz/output.
5. **Ban quyen**: khong sao chep van ban, audio, hinh anh hay bai tap cua giao trinh.
6. **Runtime**: seed transaction, API chi tra ve `PUBLISHED`, audio placeholder
   khong duoc publish.

Chi khi ca 6 lop dat moi doi `DRAFT` sang `IN_REVIEW`, sau review moi publish.
