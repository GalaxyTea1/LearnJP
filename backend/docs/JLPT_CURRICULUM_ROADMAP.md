# JLPT N5-N1 Comprehensive Curriculum Roadmap

Tai lieu nay la ban thiet ke noi dung chinh cho website. Muc tieu la khong chi
day de thi JLPT ma con giup nguoi hoc su dung duoc tieng Nhat trong giao tiep,
nghe, doc va viet.

## 1. Cau truc bat buoc cua mot module

Moi module/bai hoc phai la mot goi noi dung hoan chinh:

1. `Vocabulary`: tu moi, cach doc, loai tu, nghia va cau vi du.
2. `Kanji`: am On/Kun, bo thu, so net, tu ghep va mnemonic.
3. `Grammar`: mau cau, y nghia, cach dung, luu y va vi du.
4. `Kaiwa`: hoi thoai theo tinh huong, vai noi va bien the lich su.
5. `Listening`: audio/script, transcript, tu khoa va cau hoi nghe.
6. `Reading`: doan doc dung cap do va cau hoi doc hieu.
7. `Writing/Output`: viet cau, viet doan, ghi am hoac role-play.
8. `Practice`: bai tap nho theo tung ky nang.
9. `Module quiz`: bai kiem tra tong hop cuoi module.
10. `Review`: danh sach loi sai va noi dung dua vao SRS.

## 3. Chuan dau ra tung ky nang

### Vocabulary

- Hoc theo chu de va cum tu, khong hoc danh sach roi rac.
- Moi tu co it nhat mot cau vi du tu nhien.
- Dong tu co nhom chia; tinh tu co loai `i/na`.
- Tu N3 tro len can co dong nghia, trai nghia va sac thai.

### Kanji

- Hoc Kanji qua tu vung thuc te.
- Moi Kanji co 2-4 tu ghep uu tien.
- Khong yeu cau nguoi hoc nho tat ca am doc cung luc.
- Kanji moi phai xuat hien lai trong reading va exercise.

### Grammar

- Moi mau co cau truc, y nghia, ngu canh va loi sai thuong gap.
- N3-N1 phai co phan so sanh mau gan nghia.
- Moi mau co it nhat 3 cau vi du va 5 cau bai tap.

### Kaiwa

- Moi hoi thoai co vai noi, boi canh va muc tieu giao tiep.
- Co ban cham, ban tu nhien va phien ban role-play.
- N2-N1 phai phan biet casual, polite, sonkeigo va kenjougo.

### Listening

- Co audio, transcript, thoi luong va toc do.
- N5-N4 nghe 2 lan; N3-N1 huong toi nghe 1 lan.
- Cau hoi gom bat y chinh, chi tiet, muc dich va thai do nguoi noi.

### Reading

- N5: cau va thong bao ngan.
- N4: email, lich trinh, huong dan.
- N3: tin ngan, blog, y kien.
- N2: bao chi, bao cao, lap luan.
- N1: xa luan, hoc thuat, van hoc va noi dung chuyen mon.

### Writing va output

JLPT khong thi viet/noi, nhung website van can hai ky nang nay de nguoi hoc
su dung duoc tieng Nhat.

- N5: viet 5-8 cau va tu gioi thieu 2 phut.
- N4: viet email/nhat ky 120-180 chu va noi 5 phut.
- N3: viet tom tat/y kien 250-350 chu va noi 7 phut.
- N2: viet email cong viec/bao cao 400-600 chu va thuyet trinh 10 phut.
- N1: viet bai lap luan 800-1,200 chu va bao ve quan diem 12-15 phut.

- menu, bien bao, lich hoc;

- ho so ca nhan;
- mot doan 100-250 ky tu.

## 10. Cau truc danh gia

### Trong mot module

- mini practice sau moi phan;
- quiz 15-35 cau;
- diem dat: 80%;
- cau sai duoc dua vao danh sach on.

### Sau moi 3-4 module

- checkpoint test 40-60 cau;
- listening va reading bat buoc;
- diem dat: 70%;
- neu khong dat, he thong de xuat module can on.

### Cuoi cap

- it nhat 3 de thi thu;
- 2 de lien tiep dat 70% moi mo khoa cap tiep theo;
- hoan thanh capstone output;
- ty le on SRS dung han tu 80%.
