# Curriculum Source of Truth - N5

Tai lieu nay la hop dong noi dung giua SumaryJapanese va Gemini. Gemini khong
duoc tu suy dien chu de, ngu phap hay so luong tu moi; moi module phai duoc tao
tu mot dong syllabus da duoc khoa trong tai lieu nay.

## 1. Nguyen tac nguon tham chieu

Su dung ba lop nguon theo thu tu uu tien:

1. **JLPT competency target**: muc tieu ky nang nghe, doc va nhan biet tu vung/
   Kanji/ngu phap o cap do N5-N1.
2. **Giao trinh tham khao**: Minna no Nihongo (I, II), Irodori va Genki dung
   de tham khao thu tu su pham, tinh huong va do kho; khong sao chep nguyen van
   bai, audio, vi du, hinh anh hay bai tap co ban quyen.
3. **Syllabus cua SumaryJapanese**: danh sach pham vi bat buoc ben duoi. Day la
   nguon quyet dinh khi cac giao trinh khac nhau ve thu tu hoac cach chia bai.

JLPT khong cong bo mot danh sach tu vung/ngu phap chinh thuc day du cho tung
cap. Vi vay, moi tu va mau cau phai co `introducedIn`, `reviewedIn` va `source`
trong ledger noi dung cua du an.

## 3. Goi du lieu bat buoc cho moi module

Moi module phai co mot file brief rieng, khong tao truc tiep tu prompt chung.
File brief can khoa:

- `code`, `level`, `unit`, tien quyet va learning outcomes;
- grammar allowlist va grammar forbidden list;
- vocabulary domains, quota core/extension va danh sach tu da hoc;
- Kanji moi, readings duoc phep va tu ghep bat buoc;
- boi canh Kaiwa, do dai Listening/Reading, dau ra Writing;
- quota tung bang va mapping section;
- tieu chi dat module va cau hoi QA;
- JSON schema, enum, zero-based answer va placeholder audio.

## 4. Quy trinh dung Gemini de tao dung

Khong yeu cau Gemini tao ngay mot JSON lon. Dung hai luot:

### Luot A - Lap ke hoach

Gui syllabus row, cumulative ledger va brief. Yeu cau Gemini tra ve `plan.json`
gom danh sach tu vung, Kanji, grammar, dependencies va canh bao trung lap.
Nguoi review phe duyet plan truoc khi tao content.

### Luot B - Tao content

Chi sau khi plan duoc phe duyet, yeu cau Gemini tao JSON theo brief. Bat buoc
ghi `sourceRef` cho moi item (vi du `N5-04.vocab.013`) va `introducedIn`/
`reviewedIn`; khong tao database ID.

Sau do chay validator may va mot vong review tieng Nhat. Gemini khong duoc tu
dat status `PUBLISHED`; du lieu moi luon la `DRAFT`.

## 6. Prompt khung cho Gemini

```text
Ban la content designer cho SumaryJapanese.

Chi duoc su dung:
1) CURRICULUM_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md;
2) file brief cua module;
3) cumulative-ledger.json do du an cung cap.

Khong duoc tu them grammar, Kanji hoac tu vung ngoai allowlist. Neu can them,
dua vao `extensionCandidates` va dung tu da biet thay the. Khong sao chep
nguyen van Minna no Nihongo; hay viet vi du, hoi thoai va passage moi.

Moi item phai co sourceRef, introducedIn/reviewedIn, explanation va level.
Khong tao id, moduleId, SQL, password, token hay URL audio that.
Status luon la DRAFT. correctIndex va correctAnswer dung zero-based.

Tra ve JSON duy nhat theo schema cua brief. Neu co bat ky dieu nao khong chac
chan, dung tao du lieu im lang; ghi vao `qaWarnings`.
```

## 7. Cac lop QA truoc khi import

1. **Schema**: JSON parse, enum, required fields, foreign-key mapping.
2. **Curriculum**: dung allowlist, dung quota, khong trung cumulative ledger.
3. **Tieng Nhat**: reading, pitch/romaji, chia dong tu, tu nhien va cap do.
4. **Su pham**: learning outcomes xuat hien trong exercise/quiz/output.
5. **Ban quyen**: khong sao chep van ban, audio, hinh anh hay bai tap cua giao trinh.
6. **Runtime**: seed transaction, API chi tra ve `PUBLISHED`, audio placeholder
   khong duoc publish.

Chi khi ca 6 lop dat moi doi `DRAFT` sang `IN_REVIEW`, sau review moi publish.
